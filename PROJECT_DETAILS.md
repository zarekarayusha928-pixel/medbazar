# Med Bazar — Project Details

## Product Name
**Med Bazar**

## Product Type
Multi-pharmacy SaaS + pharmacy marketplace + pharmacy operations platform.

## Vision

To provide pharmacies with one professional system for managing their day-to-day operations while allowing customers to discover participating pharmacies and order medicines online.

## Problem

Traditional small and medium pharmacies may maintain separate processes for:

- Counter sales
- Inventory
- Online orders
- Prescription handling
- Customer records
- Staff
- Branches
- Reports

This can cause duplicate data entry, inaccurate stock, difficult reporting, and poor visibility.

## Solution

Med Bazar creates one connected system where:

- Online sales update inventory.
- Offline POS sales update the same inventory.
- Each pharmacy controls its own business data.
- Multi-branch pharmacies can manage branches centrally.
- Customers can order from participating pharmacies.
- Pharmacists can review prescription-required orders.
- Owners can monitor sales and inventory.

## Target Users

### 1. Customer
Uses the marketplace/customer application.

### 2. Pharmacy Owner
Manages one pharmacy and its branches.

### 3. Branch Manager
Manages a particular branch.

### 4. Pharmacist
Handles medicine and prescription-related workflows.

### 5. Cashier
Uses POS for physical store sales.

### 6. Delivery Staff
Handles assigned deliveries.

### 7. Platform Administrator
Operates the Med Bazar platform itself.

## Product Boundaries

Med Bazar is software infrastructure. Prescription verification, medicine dispensing, payment, delivery, licensing, and other regulated operations must follow the applicable local laws, professional requirements, and pharmacy policies.

## Success Criteria

The system should make these questions easy to answer:

- What medicines are available at this branch?
- What was sold online today?
- What was sold offline today?
- What is the current stock?
- Which medicines are low in stock?
- Which batches are near expiry?
- Which prescriptions need review?
- Which orders need processing?
- Which staff member handled an action?
- How much did the pharmacy/branch sell?
