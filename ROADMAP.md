## Recommended MVP

For the first working version, build only:

1. Authentication
2. Pharmacy + branch
3. Medicine catalogue
4. Inventory
5. POS
6. Customer medicine search
7. Cart
8. Prescription upload
9. Online order
10. Pharmacy order dashboard
11. Stock synchronization
12. Basic reports

Do not build every advanced feature at the beginning.
